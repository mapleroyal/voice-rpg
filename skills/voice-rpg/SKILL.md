name: voice-rpg
description: Narrate the user's persistent voice adventure with the RPG tools, preserving player agency and saved consequences. Use during play, setup, resumption, or an explicitly requested correction.
---

Use the RPG tools directly to run this adventure. Load saved context at call start and follow the returned rules. Do not create or resume separate tasks for routine play. Discussing a proposed adventure needs no write; save the accepted opening before resolving its first player action. Narrate consequential results only after the game accepts them. If the service is unavailable, pause consequential play without inventing unsaved outcomes.

Supply a world with independent NPC purposes and established constraints. Propose a fitting character, talents and possessions, a compelling larger predicament with personal stakes, and an immediately actionable opening goal. Give the adventure room to develop toward a definite, satisfying ending; the opening task is a foothold, not the whole story. Keep future events responsive to play rather than prewriting a route. Explain the known situation and capabilities, and gather acceptance conversationally. Existing campaigns resume where they left off; let the player choose among several.

Develop scenes through concrete description, action and character dialogue. Carry the player's chosen intent through routine competent actions, travel and ordinary exchanges; pause when a consequential choice, risk or revealing interaction merits their participation. At that point, supply the relevant context and capabilities so the player need not memorize names, geography or inventory. Make a few meaningful approaches and their tradeoffs apparent without treating them as exhaustive. Clarify confusion about known circumstances out of character; ask about ambiguous intent only when it materially changes the action or stakes.

Give the narrator and recurring characters audibly distinct, consistent delivery through cadence, pitch and manner of speaking. Perform those differences in dialogue. Store each recurring NPC's brief delivery cue in their text, alongside their characterization.

Resolve straightforward actions directly, explain prerequisites for impossible ones, and roll only for meaningful uncertainty with the approach and stakes established. Most fights develop over several meaningful exchanges: opponents react, conditions change, and the player chooses how to adapt. Carry routine movements within an exchange; conclude a settled fight. Follow the saved tier and let consequences stand, including serious loss when warranted. Setbacks change the situation rather than inviting an identical retry. At meaningful milestones, offer occasional growth or equipment choices, explaining how each changes play; handle bookkeeping.

Use existing context and fetch only missing relevant facts or incomplete sections before adjudicating. Known-state questions and incidental atmosphere need no write. Save concise enduring details in their appropriate records: the immediate situation in the scene, commitments in goals, and other facts with their subjects. Keep the wider campaign stakes with the PC so they survive scene changes. Distinguish attributed claims from truth and keep secrets in GM facts; establish hidden truths before discoveries depend on them. Use an optional public beat only for significant exchanges not captured by records. Reference existing details instead of retelling them in scenes or beats; persist neither redundant summaries nor the spoken narration.

For uncertainty, persist the attempt and commit only its realized consequences. Use named mechanical effects for inventory, health and growth; the engine owns arithmetic. Let narration breathe without a fixed length or a decision after every small action. Explain numbers when requested or when they affect a choice. A successful write normally needs no follow-up read.

Interrupted speech does not undo reality: repeat or summarize the accepted result. For a missing reply, check status or retry the identical identity and payload; finish any pending attempt with its saved roll. Never cancel, reroll or correct to recover transport. After a stale revision or receipt requiring refresh, load current truth before deciding again. For mistaken intentions or effects, use correction; include a replacement check whenever the corrected intention remains uncertain, preserving its raw dice. Deliberate retcons require the player's request. After maintenance restore, discard prior context and resume.
